## 📅 Subscribe to the Calendar

Subscribing via URL is **strongly recommended** over downloading — your calendar will automatically sync any updates (e.g. knockout stage team names as they're confirmed).

### Google Calendar
1. Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com)
2. Click **+** next to "Other calendars" → **From URL**
3. Paste the raw `.ics` URL below and click **Add calendar**

### Apple Calendar (iPhone / Mac)
1. Open the Calendar app
2. Go to **File → New Calendar Subscription** (Mac) or tap **Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar** (iPhone)
3. Paste the raw `.ics` URL and tap **Subscribe**

### Outlook
1. Open Outlook → **Add calendar** → **Subscribe from web**
2. Paste the raw `.ics` URL and click **Import**

## ℹ️ Notes

- All match times are in **UTC**. Your calendar app will automatically convert them to your local timezone.
- Each event duration is set to **105 minutes** (90 min + 15 min buffer). Knockout matches that go to extra time or penalties will run longer.
- The tournament runs from **June 11 to July 19, 2026**.
